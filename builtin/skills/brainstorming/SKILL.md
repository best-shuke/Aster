# Brainstorming

Use when the solution space is open (product directions, names, architectures) and an
early favourite would narrow it too soon.

## Run it

`start_protocol protocol=brainstorm topic=... participants=[...] goal="criteria"`

The runtime runs two rounds:

1. **Blind round**: each participant proposes ideas without seeing anyone else's.
   Messaging between participants is blocked until everyone has contributed.
2. **Build round**: everyone sees all ideas and combines, extends or critiques them.

The facilitator then concludes with a shortlist.

## Good contributions

- Blind round: 3-7 distinct ideas, each with one line on why it could work. Include at
  least one unconventional idea. Quantity and spread over polish.
- Build round: merge overlapping ideas, name the strongest two or three against the
  criteria, and state the main risk of each.
- Conclusion: a ranked shortlist with criteria scores, the rejected ideas worth keeping in
  mind, and tasks to validate the top choice.

Without the protocol, you can still brainstorm alone: write many options first, then
evaluate. Use the protocol when diversity of perspective matters.
